Statutory right of withdrawal

Withdrawal is distinct from cancellation. Where a statutory right of withdrawal applies, the period for an online service contract is generally 14 days from conclusion. Statutory exceptions may apply depending on the type of contract and the declarations made at purchase. These terms do not restrict your right of withdrawal.

  • Apple: Apple explains how to withdraw through your purchase history and lists other ways to submit a withdrawal in Section B of its terms for Austria and Germany.
